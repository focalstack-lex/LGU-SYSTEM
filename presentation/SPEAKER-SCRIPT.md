## Slide 1: Asa Kaya Napadulong ang Pondo?

**On slide:** Hook question + live dashboard screenshot.

>
> Everything you'll see today is the real system. The dashboard on the right is not a mock-up. This is what any student sees when they open our portal: income, expenses, balance, live.
>
> This is the **COE Budget Transparency and Financial Monitoring System**, and its entire purpose is to make sure that question always has an answer."

*[Pause after the question. Let it sit for 2 seconds.]*

## Slide 2: The Problem

**On slide:** 3 numbered pains + the "2 to 3 weeks" stat.

> "Right now, most student organizations, and most local offices, manage money the same way. **Paper receipts** that fade, fold, and vanish before audit season. **Manual spreadsheets** that take forever to reconcile and are easy to get wrong. And **zero visibility**: the members whose funds these are never see the breakdown.
>
> Point to the number: one event liquidation used to take **two to three weeks**. So when someone asks where the funds went, nobody can answer quickly, and that's how distrust starts. It's not that money is missing. It's that *proof* is missing."

---

## Slide 3: The System

**On slide:** Login screenshot + Events screenshot, side by side.

> "Our answer is one system with two faces.
>
> For **students**, a secure portal signed in through your official CJC Google account. The budget is live and public: income, expenses, and every event, like the page on the right. Budget versus actual spending, per event. Nothing to request, nothing to wait for.
>
> For **officers**, the same system is a recording console: every transaction is entered with a **photo of the receipt**. If you can post a photo, you can use it.
>
> It runs in any browser, works on phones, installs as an app, and even works offline."

---

## Slide 4: What's Inside

**On slide:** 5 numbered features + Grizz AI panel. *(Walk the list, one sentence each.)*

> "Five things the system does:
>
> - A **real-time dashboard**: totals update the moment a transaction is saved.
> - An **events and budgets tracker**: each event gets its own envelope; you see budget against actual spending.
> - A **digital receipt vault**: every transaction carries its own photo proof.
> - **One-click reports**: audit-ready documents generated instantly, not compiled for weeks.
> - And our favorite: **Grizz**, the AI copilot on the right. That answer is generated live from the actual ledger. You ask in plain language, 'How much did we spend on Engineering Week?', and it answers."

>
> The picture on the right is what that looks like in practice: every peso carries its own official receipt, stored digitally. No fading, no losing. Overall, reporting time dropped by about **80%**, and lost documents are now **zero**."

## Quick Q&A Prep

**"Is it secure? Who can edit the data?"**
→ Only authorized officers, through role-based access. Students see a read-only view. Every action is logged in an audit trail, so we always know *who* recorded *what* and *when*.

**"What if there's no internet?"**
→ It's installable as an offline-capable app (PWA). Core pages still load, and data syncs when back online.

**"Is that data on the slides real?"**
→ The screenshots are the actual system; the figures shown are sample data for demonstration. The ₱150,000 liquidation case is real: 26 days before, 3 minutes after.

**"How long did it take to build / how hard was it?"**
→ It's fully working and deployed today. A web app with a proper database and role-based access, not a prototype.

**"Can other organizations adopt it?"**
→ That's the point. The design mirrors how LGUs handle public funds, so any organization that handles money can use the same model.
