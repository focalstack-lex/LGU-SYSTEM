# Student walkthrough: what a third-year BSCoE student meets (2026-09-28)

Method: `node scripts/student-walkthrough.mjs` drives the portal as a plain `student` account (Alex Reyes, BSCoE, 3rd year, verified) with a realistic record (12 passed subjects, 1 failed, 1 enrolled, a draft load) against the mocked backend, at 1440px and 390px. It performs the actions a student takes: opens an event, searches the ledger, views a receipt, adds and removes a subject, submits the load, logs a subject, opens the profile, opens Grizz, and browses the phone "More" sheet. 80 observations, screenshots and page text are in `reports/ui-verification/2026-09-28-student-walkthrough/`. Findings were then checked against the code; items that came from the mock data (dashes in seeded descriptions, the empty search result, the CV builder 401, the "Total Income" loader) are excluded.

Branch: `ui/soft-palette` (includes everything released to `main` today). No code was changed for this walkthrough.

## What already works well for a student

Sign-in is Google-first with a clear password fallback. The ledger is fully readable (dates, event, amount, who added it, receipt link) with no edit or delete controls leaking (0 found). Event cards show budget bars and over-budget notes. The enrollment flow's four-step tracker reads clearly, adding and removing subjects is one click, and submission locks the load and shows a toast. Academic Progress lists every subject by year and semester with status and grade, and the year filter and PDF download work. The profile modal traps focus and closes on Escape. The phone layout has no horizontal overflow on any view, and the bottom nav items are 48px tall.
