# Legal, IP & Accessibility Compliance Audit Report (/copyright)

- Project: LGU System - College of Engineering Official Student Portal
- Date: 2026-09-24
- Stack: HTML5 / JavaScript (ES6) / CSS3 / Supabase / Express
- Scope: Project-wide legal compliance with targeted Login surface enforcement

## Executive Summary

A comprehensive legal risk and compliance audit was performed across seven legal lenses. Per user instruction, legal copyright disclosures and privacy terms were applied exclusively to login entry points.

## Per-Lens Findings & Citations

### Lens 1: Intellectual Property & Trademark Protection
- [P2] Missing IP & Copyright Statement on Authentication Gates
  - Location: `client/index.html:98-105`, `client/feedback/index.html:35-38`
  - Risk: Lack of explicit copyright ownership statement on main authentication interfaces.
  - Status: Remediated. Applied copyright notice directly inside login form containers.

### Lens 2: Website Accessibility (ADA Title III & WCAG 2.1 AA)
- [P3] Non-specific Image Alt Attribute
  - Location: `client/index.html:45`
  - Risk: Screen reader users receive generic label ("COE Logo") instead of full institutional description.
  - Recommendation: Update alt attribute to "College of Engineering Official Seal".

### Lens 3: Data Privacy, Cybersecurity & Cookies (GDPR / Data Privacy Act of 2012)
- [P1] Login Form Collecting Student Credentials Without Direct Privacy Policy Link
  - Location: `client/index.html:61-101`
  - Risk: Non-compliance with Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) Section 16 requiring explicit data subject notification prior to processing credentials.
  - Status: Remediated. Injected active Privacy Policy and Terms of Service references directly into the login submission container.

### Lens 4: E-Commerce & Consumer Protection Laws
- [P3] Student Fee Display Transparency Notice
  - Location: `client/index.html:51-53`
  - Recommendation: Ensure financial transparency disclaimers state official receipt policies on payment collection screens.

### Lens 5: Product Liability & Safety Advisories
- [P3] Security Account Notice
  - Location: `client/index.html:92-95`
  - Risk: Minimal. Security advisory warning users against submitting official GSuite passwords is line-injected and compliant.

### Lens 6: Communications & Anti-Spam Compliance (CAN-SPAM / TCPA)
- [P2] Missing Opt-in Consent Disclosure for Automated Notifications
  - Location: `client/index.html:107-160`
  - Risk: Automated email or SMS announcements dispatched upon registration require explicit opt-in confirmation during student onboarding.

### Lens 7: Tax & Financial Transparency
- [P3] Institutional Council Fee Audit Disclaimers
  - Location: `client/js/app.js:1-100`
  - Recommendation: Clearly mark non-refundable fee breakdowns in council financial reports.
