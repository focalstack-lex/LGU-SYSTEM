## Phone app shell fills the viewport (branch: ui/nav-consolidation)

- [17:21] Reported from an iPhone 11: after login the portal ends in a cut band at the bottom instead of filling the screen. Root cause: `#app-screen` is a fixed `height: 100vh; overflow: hidden` shell (`main.css`), and on iOS Safari 100vh is the toolbar-hidden height, so with the toolbar visible the shell runs behind it and the document stays scrollable by the difference; the auth screen on phones is a scrolling document, so a leftover scroll offset or the open keyboard from the login form shows as a band under the shell once the app screen appears. Fix: `#app-screen` declares `height: 100dvh` after the 100vh fallback, and `UI.showScreen` (`ui.js`) now blurs the active element and resets `window.scrollTo(0, 0)` on every screen switch. Cache-busters: main.css 8.3 on all four pages that load it, ui.js 5.12. New `scripts/verify-app-shell-fill.mjs` emulates iPhone 11, iPhone SE and a 360px Android, scrolls the auth document before switching, and asserts the shell starts at 0, ends at the viewport bottom, the document does not overflow, scrollY is 0 and the dvh rule is declared.
