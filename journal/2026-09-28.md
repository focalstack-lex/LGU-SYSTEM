## Dropdown placement and broken icons (branch: ui/audit-fixes)

- [13:00] **Dropdown bug from the reported screenshot.** The enrollment year menu overlapped its trigger, hid its last option and ran off the right edge. There were three root causes:
  - The animated views (`.view` and `.of-view`, with `will-change: transform`) become the containing block for `position: fixed`, so menu coordinates were measured from the view, not the viewport. The desktop officer menus were shifted by the 252px sidebar.
  - `max-height: 180px` was shorter than a 5-option list (about 205px).
  - `.enrollment-select-wrapper .dd-menu { width: 100% !important }` made the fixed menu span the whole viewport.
- [13:00] **Dropdown fix in `client/js/dropdown.js`:**
  - The menu measures the real fixed origin with a probe and anchors by top in both directions.
  - It sizes to its content up to 320px and opens toward the side that fits.
  - It reserves the resting position of the auto-hiding mobile bottom nav.
  - It fits its width to its options, marking the (bold) selection before measuring, and scrolls the selection into view.
  - CSS: an upward-open animation, and the conflicting enrollment `!important` width and z-index removed.
- [13:00] **Icons.** Commit `b6844bd` had removed `vendor/icons-bundle.js` from `index.html` and `faculty.html`, so the whole student and faculty portals fetched icons from api.iconify.design and went blank offline. Nine icon names in use do not exist in the Solar set (`academic-cap`, `vault`, `table`, `check-bold`, `document-edit`, `file-upload` x2, `video-camera-off`, and the runtime-built `caution` and `search`), so they were always blank. Fixes:
  - Restored the bundle tag on both pages.
  - Replaced the nonexistent names with valid bundled ones.
  - Added `scripts/generate-icon-bundle.mjs` (`npm run icons:bundle` / `icons:check`), which builds the bundle from the icons the source actually uses and refuses nonexistent names. It replaces the drifting hand list in `scratch/generate_icon_bundle.js`.
